The dye room

Four hundred metres a month, and not one hurry

The atelier still works under the 1931 roof on rue de Sévigné. Skeins are dyed in small baths against a kept book of recipes, so a colour missed in November can be matched again in March.

The looms run to a hand’s measure; nothing about the house runs on a season’s clock.
