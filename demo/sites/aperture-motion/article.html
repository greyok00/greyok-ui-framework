notes from set · entry 12 — permits & windows

How a permit day holds: the golden hour is a contract.

JV By June Valdez, line producer · 2 Nov 2026 · 7 min read

A permit day is not a wish for good weather — it is a signed window with a clock in it. This is how Halide plans a day so the sun, the county, and the crew all land on the same side of it.

The window is the schedule

Every Halide day is written around two clocks: the permit window and the light window. The permit says when we may shoot; the sun says when we should. On The Salt Ledger the two agreed exactly once — day 31 — and that day produced the flat's only wide on the water.

Call sheets are published at 04:58, before any unit member is awake, with the sunset block at the top of page one because it is the one thing nobody may argue with.

The unit's keys

The log lives in the shoot-log desk. Unit keys, learned by muscle memory in week one:
