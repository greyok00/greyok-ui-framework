We keep the quiet designs.

Studio Ma is a design gallery and studio archive: four hundred eleven printed artifacts, nine exhibitions a year, and one rule we never break — a thing is finished when nothing can be taken from it.

Identity

Letterheads of the northern kilns

Forty-one letterheads commissioned between 1989 and 2004 by the ceramic workshops along the Kamo river. Two survive in use; thirty-nine live here.

Ma Editions, numbers 1 to 60

Sixty cloth-bound volumes, printed at sixty copies each, never reprinted. Number 19, Rules of the Quiet House, is currently on view.

Posters

The moon calendar series

Ninety lithographs printed each winter solstice from 1998. The 2011 sheet was pulled during a power failure and is the only one signed blind.
