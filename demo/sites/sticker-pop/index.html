New season · The Bumblefluffs

Silly little films. Serious big feelings.

Wiggleworks is a nine-person animation studio in Portland, Oregon. We make The Bumblefluffs — four round bee-beasts and their very patient neighbor Mrs. Peach — as film shorts, sticker packs, and picture books.
