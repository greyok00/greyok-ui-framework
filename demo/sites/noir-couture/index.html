Standing rules · pinned at the deskUnamended since 1998

The desk corresponds; it does not sell, hurry, or grant. Three rules hold, whatever the season asks: the plates release with the hour and not before it; an embargo once given is kept, and correspondents who wait are served first the next edition; seats are written by hand, in one book, at one desk.
