Key findings

01

Grind distribution, not dose alone, drove shot consistency: a burr upgrade cut swing shots from about one in four to one in ten across a 9-week log.

02

Machines held temperature within 0.5C only after warmup flushes; every graded source that measured straight startup shots logged wider swings.

03

Fresh roast dates mattered more than bag price: beans roasted within 14 days beat every older premium bag on the blind cupping scores we kept.
